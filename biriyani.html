Southery / Biriyani

Malabar dum biriyani, cooked in Edappally

Our biriyani is made the Malabar way: short-grain Kaima rice layered with the masala and the meat, sealed, and left on dum until every grain carries it.

Chicken and beef biriyani are always on the menu. Mutton, fish and prawns biriyani are cooked for direct orders only. Mutton biriyani is priced below; for fish and prawns, message us on WhatsApp for sizes and prices.

How to order

  1. Add dishes to your cart on southery.cloud.
  2. Send the order to our kitchen on WhatsApp. It goes as one ready-written message.
  3. Pay by UPI. We start cooking once the payment comes through.

Delivery is free within 3 km of our Edappally kitchen. Further away, we send it with Porter once your payment comes through, and you pay the rider.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 11 am to 11 pm. Closed on Mondays. Also on Swiggy and Zomato.
