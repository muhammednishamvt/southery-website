Our story

We didn’t set out to reinvent tradition. We set out to serve it properly.

This started with missing home. With food that tasted the way it did growing up, and the slow realisation that it was getting harder to find.

We kept the recipes as we received them. From home kitchens, from people who never wrote anything down.

We’re not trying to be everywhere. We’re trying to be the kitchen you come back to, because what arrives is exactly what was promised.

Vishnu DSFounder, Southery

Where we stand

Between the heavy old menu and the next food trend, there is a kitchen that simply cooks Malabar properly.

Not a restaurant menu, shrunk for delivery.No forty-page menus, no dishes from every corner of the country.
SoutheryOne cuisine, done properly. A focused Malabar menu, cooked fresh and built for the journey to your door.
Not a trend.No gimmicks, no fusion for the sake of it, nothing made for a feed instead of a table.

  1. 01

    Rooted

    Recipes as we received them.

    Every dish has a reason to be on our menu, and every recipe comes from Malabar home kitchens. Our biriyani is Kaima rice and dum cooked, the way it has always been made.

  2. 02

    Honest

    What arrives is what was promised.

    Our ingredients are sourced locally. The photographs on this page are of our own food, cooked in our own kitchen. Nothing is dressed up to look like something it isn’t.

  3. 03

    Calm

    Cooked at the pace the food needs.

    Dum biriyani is sealed and left to cook in its own steam. Roasts are taken down slowly. We don’t cut that time to cook faster, and we don’t shout about what we make.

  4. 04

    Crafted

    Every detail, down to the box.

    From the portion on the plate to the paper bag at your door, everything is considered. Packed in paper and cornstarch containers, sealed for the road.

The kitchen
