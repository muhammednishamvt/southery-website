Vishnu DS

Founder, Southery

We didn’t set out to reinvent tradition. We set out to serve it properly.

This started with missing home. With food that tasted the way it did growing up, and the slow realisation that it was getting harder to find.

We kept the recipes as we received them. From home kitchens, from people who never wrote anything down.

We’re not trying to be everywhere. We’re trying to be the kitchen you come back to, because what arrives is exactly what was promised.

Passed down. Made with love.

Not a forty-page menu shrunk for delivery, and not the next food trend. A focused Malabar kitchen, built around four things we hold to.

Rooted
Recipes as we received them, from Malabar home kitchens. Our biriyani is Kaima rice and dum cooked, the way it has always been made.
Honest
Ingredients sourced locally. Every photograph here is our own food from our own kitchen. What arrives is what was promised.
Calm
Food cooked at the pace it needs. Dum biriyani is sealed and left alone; roasts are taken down slowly. We don’t cut that time.
Crafted
Considered down to the box: paper bags and cornstarch containers, sealed so your order arrives the way it left.
