How to order

  1. Add dishes to your cart on southery.cloud.
  2. Send the order to our kitchen on WhatsApp. It goes as one ready-written message.
  3. Pay by UPI. We start cooking once the payment comes through.

Delivery is free within 3 km of our Edappally kitchen. Further away, we send it with Porter once your payment comes through, and you pay the rider.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 11 am to 11 pm. Closed on Mondays. Also on Swiggy and Zomato.
